# IG file imports

IG uses files only; this integration never stores an IG password or calls the trading API.
Each spread betting and share dealing account remains separate and reports native GBP cash.

In Broker Sync, use **Update your IG accounts**. Select one or more registered accounts, choose the requested full-history CSVs and both monthly PDFs, and select **Preview import**. The server reads cash and holdings from the trading statement and validates the ledger account identity. Review the reconciled latest cash balances and new-record counts, then select **Import checked reports**. A private full-site backup is required and taken automatically before applying. Preview tokens belong to the logged-in user, expire after 15 minutes and are invalidated by account updates. Raw PDF uploads are processed in memory; parsed history is retained in the private database for future comparisons. Include both accounts when a new internal transfer appears.

Export full account history on each import. Spread betting requires Transactions, Past activity and P&L Breakdown CSVs. Share dealing requires Transactions CSV and the holdings/cash statement. Keep the monthly ledger PDF and trading statement PDF as private reconciliation evidence. Statement cutoffs may precede interest payments present in the CSV.

The operator prepares a private JSON array of account inputs for `backend/scripts/import-ig-statements.js`. Each input has `name`, a stable account `identity`, `kind` (`spread_bet` or `share_dealing`), CSV text in `transactions`, and `confirmation` containing verified `cash`, UTC `cutoff` and `holdings`. Spread betting also supplies `breakdown` and `activity`. Share holdings contain `name`, `symbol`, `isin`, `quantity`, `cost`, `value` and dated `asOf`. These confirmations must come from inspected statements, never inferred current quotes. Keep input files outside Git; inside Docker use the private application data volume.

Run with `PRIVATE_JSON USER_ID --dry-run` first, then `--apply` after a database backup. The dry run rolls back accounts, trades, cash events, transfer links and portfolio snapshots. Historical FX rates may be cached separately. A repeat import skips matching records; changed financial records, missing full history, ambiguous transfers, missing FX, unsupported transaction categories or unreconciled balances stop the whole import.

GBP-settled share purchases and sales use the trading statement's **GBP Account Activity** table and the full CSV settlement history. `CONS quantity@price` is a contract settlement descriptor, not proof of a corporate consolidation. Executions are matched by deal code, quantity, price and exact cash; the trade date is distinct from the CSV settlement date. GBP versus pence price units must reconcile to the reported amount including dealing charges. Contract evidence is retained privately across monthly updates. FIFO handles partial and full sales, preserving the original journal ID on a final close. Lot quantities and gross acquisition costs must reconcile to statement holdings; settled costs and proceeds include charges for net P&L. Unknown share ISINs retain their ISIN as the display symbol until ticker identity is established. New acquisitions and sales require the statement containing their execution. Non-GBP executions and unsupported table layouts stop for review. Existing statement-confirmed seed holdings can be retained without an original contract note, but their journal date remains the settlement date until the actual execution is supplied.

Spread-bet daily trading statements with the **Financial Current Open Positions** table are supported. The table is reconciled to reported long/short notional exposure and running P&L, then checked against the full Past Activity and closed P&L CSVs. Partial closes leave only the remaining GBP stake open. Later partial closes update that stake in place; a full close reuses its journal ID for the final closed portion, retaining notes and attachments. The statement must agree with the current activity exports; an older statement cannot overwrite a newer imported checkpoint. Open P&L remains unrealised. Position quotes come from the dated IG statement, never the underlying stock quote. Notional exposure is excluded from owned stock/crypto investment value. Cash interest, dividends and funding remain actual cashflow entries; open-position funding stays in cashflow until the closing breakdown allocates it.

Spread-bet quantity means GBP stake per point. Cashflow uses actual cash settlements rather than price times stake. Journal P&L includes broker-reported funding, borrowing and stop fees; dividends and cash interest are separate income. Internal transfers affect each account cash balance but never external deposits, withdrawals or income. Unique simultaneous transfers are matched first; remaining unique opposite entries can match across an overnight posting delay of up to 24 hours. Holdings use dated statement valuations, not live prices.
